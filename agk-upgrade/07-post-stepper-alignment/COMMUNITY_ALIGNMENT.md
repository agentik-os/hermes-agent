# Collective and Community Alignment

## Boundary

Collective is the canonical human-network and collaboration universe under AD-314. Community remains the bounded object family for Space, Channel, Post, Thread, Message, Room, Event, Group, Reaction and ModerationAction. It is not the Hermes gateway session model and does not create duplicate Member or Organization identity.

## Hermes reuse

Gateway adapters can provide transport and AI participant execution. They do not become Community records or permissions. AGK stamps Human and Agent identities, applies space policy and stores durable social objects in the control plane.

Shared platform and intelligence services are dependencies of Learn. Community does not create a separate identity, shell, object graph or Runtime.

## AI participants

AI participants are clearly labeled. Tutor or documentation Agents can answer, summarize and surface resources. Important moderation decisions require governed review.

## Deals bridge

A Community Post becomes an Opportunity only through explicit user action or consented qualification. Private conversation is never silently mined into commercial leads.

## Privacy

Support public, membership, cohort, Project, Organization, private and direct scopes. Blocking, reporting, removal and audit logs are control-plane capabilities.

## Priority

P2 bounded foundation. Voice, video, coworking and large-scale moderation are later. Existing messaging infrastructure must be evaluated before any realtime rebuild.
